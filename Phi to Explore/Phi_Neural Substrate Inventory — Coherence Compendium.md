# Neural Substrate Inventory — Coherence Compendium

## REGISTER 1: DIRECT OBSERVATION
(Empirical findings — measured, recorded, replicated in controlled conditions. The evidence speaks for itself.)

### N1. Pletzer, Kerschbaum & Klimesch (2010)
- **Citation:** Brain Research 1335:91–102, "When frequencies never synchronize: The golden mean and the resting EEG"
- **Finding:** EEG frequency bands (delta, theta, alpha, beta, gamma) form a geometric series with ratio φ ≈ 1.618. Crucially: "a synchronization of the excitatory phases of two oscillations with frequencies f1 and f2 is impossible (in a mathematical sense) when their ratio equals the golden mean, because their excitatory phases never meet." Therefore: "there is no other frequency relationship that is better capable of avoiding spurious cross-frequency coupling."
- **What is directly observed:** The resting human brain organizes its oscillatory bands at φ-ratios. This is a measured, published, peer-reviewed finding in a neuroscience journal.
- **Caveat (from compendium):** Has not been independently replicated at the scale of large neuroimaging studies. Recommended as best available evidence, not settled science.

### N2. Weiss & Weiss (2003) — predecessor to N1
- **Citation:** Chaos, Solitons & Fractals 18(4):643, "The golden mean as clock cycle of brain waves"
- **Finding:** "The metric of brain waves can always be understood as a superposition of n harmonics times 2Φ … the golden mean Φ (=1.618) as the point of resonance."
- **What is directly observed:** Theoretical/mathematical predecessor establishing the φ-resonance framework for brain wave analysis.

### N3. Tort, Komorowski, Manns, Kopell & Eichenbaum (2009)
- **Citation:** PNAS 106:20942
- **Finding:** "Theta–gamma coupling increases during the learning of item–context associations."
- **What is directly observed:** Hippocampal theta-gamma oscillatory coupling is measurably enhanced during associative memory encoding. This is a direct neural observation of the rhythmic substrate of memory formation.

### N4. HeartMath — McCraty, Atkinson, Tomasino & Bradley (2009) + 2025 replication
- **Citation:** "Science of the Heart," HeartMath Research Center, 2009; 2025 Scientific Reports doi: 10.1038/s41598-025-87729-7 (analysis of 1.8 million user sessions)
- **Finding:** Psychophysiological coherence centers at ~0.10 Hz (10-second rhythm), at which heart, brain, respiration, and blood-pressure rhythms entrain to a common frequency. The 2025 large-scale analysis confirms: "The most common frequency associated with Coherence was identified at 0.10 Hz."
- **What is directly observed:** A measurable, reproducible physiological state of cross-system entrainment at a specific frequency.

### N5. Yetkin et al. (2018)
- **Citation:** "A novel geometrical analysis of the arterial pulse based on the golden ratio φ: association with heart rate variability"
- **Finding:** Direct geometric φ-ratios in arterial pressure-wave decomposition (systolic/diastolic ejection-duration ratios).
- **What is directly observed:** φ-proportions in the geometry of the cardiac pulse waveform, correlated with heart rate variability.

### N6. Rolls (2013)
- **Citation:** Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience 7:74
- **Finding:** "The CA3 stage acts as an autoassociation memory which enables episodic memories to be formed and stored in the CA3 network, and that subsequently the extensive RC [recurrent collateral] connectivity allows for the retrieval of a whole representation to be initiated by the activation of some small part of the same representation (the recall cue)."
- **What is directly observed:** Hippocampal CA3 region performs autoassociative pattern completion — whole memory retrieved from partial input. Directly measured neural architecture.

### N7. Horner, Bisby, Bush, Lin & Burgess (2015)
- **Citation:** Nature Communications 6:7462
- **Finding:** "Pattern completion [is] the retrieval of all constituent elements of an event (that is, the complete pattern) when presented with a single element as a cue (that is, a partial input)."
- **What is directly observed:** Empirically confirmed neural mechanism of pattern completion in human memory, published in Nature Communications.

### N8. Klimesch (2013) — follow-on to N1
- **Citation:** Frontiers in Human Neuroscience 7:766
- **Finding:** Extension of the φ-EEG band framework, building on Pletzer et al. 2010.
- **What is directly observed:** Continued development of the φ-tuned oscillatory framework in peer-reviewed neuroscience.

## REGISTER 2: TECHNICAL SUPPORT
(Theoretical frameworks, computational models, and clinical frameworks that provide the structural architecture for interpreting the direct observations. Not raw data, but rigorous scaffolding.)

### T1. Marr (1971) — Computational Model
- **Citation:** "Simple memory: a theory for archicortex," Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B 262:23–81
- **Role:** Original computational model of hippocampal CA3 as an autoassociative memory. This is the theoretical architecture that N6 and N7 empirically confirm 40+ years later.
- **Technical contribution:** Establishes the mathematical framework for pattern completion as a computable function of neural architecture.

### T2. Buzsáki, Rhythms of the Brain (Oxford, 2006)
- **Role:** Establishes the theoretical framework for oscillatory coupling as the substrate of memory encoding and retrieval. Theta-gamma coupling (N3) is grounded in Buzsáki's broader theory of brain rhythms.
- **Technical contribution:** Provides the oscillatory architecture within which φ-tuned EEG bands (N1) operate.

### T3. Lisman & Jensen (2013)
- **Citation:** Neuron 77(6):1002
- **Role:** Theta-gamma coupling as the rhythmic substrate of sequential memory encoding and retrieval.
- **Technical contribution:** Mechanistic bridge between oscillatory coherence and episodic memory formation.

### T4. Porges, Polyvagal Theory (1994; reviewed 2022)
- **Citation:** Frontiers in Integrative Neuroscience 16:871227
- **Role:** "Feelings of safety emerge from internal physiological states regulated by the autonomic nervous system." Neuroception is the body's pre-conscious detection of safety/threat cues. Coherence in the ventral-vagal complex is the substrate of social engagement.
- **Technical contribution:** Provides the autonomic nervous system architecture that explains why physiological coherence (N4) enables social and cognitive accessibility.
- **Caveat (from compendium):** Critiqued by Grossman and Taylor for issues of falsifiability; Porges responded in Clinical Neuropsychiatry (2025). Clinical utility widely accepted; evolutionary mechanism debated.

### T5. Siegel, Interpersonal Neurobiology (1999)
- **Citation:** The Developing Mind; drdansiegel.com
- **Role:** "Integration is seen as the essential mechanism of health as it promotes a flexible and adaptive way of being … The absence of integration leads to chaos and rigidity." Integration = linkage of differentiated elements. Mental health = differentiation + linkage.
- **Technical contribution:** Provides the developmental neuroscience framework for coherence as a health metric — not merely a physical state but a relational and psychological one.

### T6. Levine, Somatic Experiencing
- **Citation:** Referenced in SF YOGA MAG
- **Role:** "I learned about coherence, or the optimal operation of breath and pulse rate, which is now studied as heart rate variability." Trauma recovery = "developing a coherence between the body and mind."
- **Technical contribution:** Clinical framework connecting physiological coherence (N4) to somatic trauma recovery. Bridges the gap between measurable HRV and lived therapeutic experience.

### T7. Khoudary et al. (2025)
- **Citation:** Cognition (2025), "Causal coherence improves episodic memory"
- **Role:** Demonstrates that narrative causal coherence enhances episodic memory encoding.
- **Technical contribution:** Extends pattern completion (N6, N7) from neural architecture to cognitive function — coherence at the narrative level improves the accessibility of memory.

### T8. PMC2254470 (2007)
- **Citation:** Phil Trans R Soc B, "Coherence and recurrency: maintenance, control and integration in working memory"
- **Role:** Technical framework for coherence as a working memory mechanism.
- **Technical contribution:** Establishes coherence as a functional property of working memory, not only long-term episodic memory.

## REGISTER 3: INTERPRETIVE SYNTHESIS
(Where the evidence ends and the naming begins. These are the moves that connect the neural substrate to the thesis — legitimate interpretive leaps, clearly marked as such.)

### I1. The φ-EEG → "Accessible Technology" Bridge
- **The leap:** From the Pletzer finding (N1) that φ-tuned EEG bands prevent destructive interference, to the claim that "coherence functions as the accessible technology of remembering."
- **What the evidence supports:** The brain organizes its oscillatory architecture at φ-ratios to prevent cross-frequency interference. This is a measured fact.
- **What is interpretive:** That this organizational principle constitutes a "technology" — a designed or designable mechanism — and that it is "accessible," meaning it can be consciously engaged. The evidence shows the brain does this; it does not show that humans can deliberately invoke it.
- **Honest framing:** "The brain's φ-tuned oscillatory architecture [N1] functions as a natural mechanism for coherent multi-frequency operation. Whether this mechanism can be consciously accessed and applied is the interpretive extension that this framework proposes."

### I2. Pattern Completion → "Remembering as Re-Gathering"
- **The leap:** From the neural mechanism of hippocampal pattern completion (N6, N7) to the contemplative and philosophical concept of "remembering" as anamnesis and re-gathering (Hexagram 59, Plato).
- **What the evidence supports:** The hippocampus retrieves whole patterns from partial cues. This is a measured neural fact.
- **What is interpretive:** That this neural mechanism is structurally isomorphic with the contemplative concept of anamnesis — that "remembering" in the deepest sense is the same operation as hippocampal pattern completion, scaled to the level of identity and meaning.
- **Honest framing:** "Neural pattern completion [N6, N7] provides the biological substrate for what contemplative traditions call anamnesis. The structural isomorphism is real; the claim that they are the same phenomenon at different scales is the interpretive move."

### I3. HeartMath 0.10 Hz → "Coherence as Accessible"
- **The leap:** From the measured 0.10 Hz entrainment frequency (N4) to the claim that coherence is "accessible" — that it can be deliberately entered through practice.
- **What the evidence supports:** A measurable physiological state of cross-system entrainment exists and centers at 0.10 Hz. This is confirmed across 1.8 million sessions.
- **What is interpretive:** That this state is accessible through intentional practice (breathing, attention, HeartMath biofeedback techniques). The evidence supports the existence of the state; the accessibility claim is supported by HeartMath's clinical practice data but is not a controlled laboratory finding.
- **Honest framing:** "The 0.10 Hz coherence state [N4] is measurable and reproducible. HeartMath's clinical practice data suggests it is accessible through intentional breathing and attention practices, though this accessibility claim rests on practitioner data rather than controlled laboratory conditions."

### I4. McGilchrist's Coherence/Correspondence → Epistemological Architecture
- **The leap:** From McGilchrist's neurological description of left/right hemisphere processing to a prescriptive epistemology for the manuscript.
- **What the evidence supports:** The left hemisphere prioritizes internal coherence (self-consistency within a sign system); the right hemisphere seeks correspondence with external reality. Integration requires both.
- **What is interpretive:** That this neurological description should govern how the manuscript itself is structured — that the manuscript must pass a "correspondence test," not merely a "coherence test."
- **Honest framing:** "McGilchrist's neurological framework [T5 in the broader compendium] describes how the brain processes coherence differently in each hemisphere. The prescription that a manuscript should mirror this integration — passing both coherence and correspondence tests — is an interpretive application of the neuroscience, not a finding of it."

### I5. Siegel's Integration Formula → HDM Health Metric
- **The leap:** From Siegel's clinical definition (mental health = differentiation + linkage) to HDM's framework for mapping invisible patterns of internal transformation.
- **What the evidence supports:** Integration of differentiated neural elements is the clinical marker of psychological health and flexibility.
- **What is interpretive:** That HDM's mapping of "trust velocity," "creative resonance," and "surface tension" can be understood as measures of Siegel's integration — that HDM operationalizes what Siegel names but does not quantify.
- **Honest framing:** "Siegel's interpersonal neurobiology [T5] defines mental health as the integration of differentiated elements. HDM's framework proposes to map the geometry of this integration — to give Siegel's qualitative definition a coordinate system. This is the interpretive extension HDM makes."

### I6. Polyvagal Safety → "Accessible" as a Physiological Condition
- **The leap:** From Porges' finding that social engagement requires ventral-vagal coherence to the claim that "accessibility" in the manuscript's thesis is a physiological state, not merely a cognitive one.
- **What the evidence supports:** The autonomic nervous system must be in a coherent (ventral-vagal) state for social engagement and learning to be possible.
- **What is interpretive:** That "accessible" in "accessible technology of remembering" refers specifically to this physiological condition — that remembering is only accessible when the nervous system is coherent.
- **Honest framing:** "Polyvagal theory [T4] establishes that social engagement and learning require a specific autonomic state. The claim that 'accessibility' in the thesis is a physiological condition — not merely a cognitive or intentional one — is an interpretive synthesis of Porges' framework with the pattern completion literature."
