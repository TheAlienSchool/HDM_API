# Glossary of Dynamical Emergence
**An Experiential Lexicon for Human Development Mathematics**

This glossary translates formal categorical mathematics into the tactile, bodily presence of human development. It maps the physics of transformation for the experiential mathematician.

---

### The Arc
**The Physics:** The angle of change. The measurable trajectory of a system moving from one state of coherence to another.
**The State:** The felt experience of being in motion. The recognition that the destination is revealed through the relationship with the trajectory itself. 

### Braiding (Braided Tensor Category)
**The Physics:** A mathematical structure where the combination of two objects encodes the history and sequence of their interaction (A crossing over B is distinct from B crossing over A).
**The State:** The accumulation of lived experience where the order of events matters. The somatic memory that every new encounter weaves into the existing structure, leaving a permanent, trackable pattern in the body. The "habit of becoming" made visible.

### Categorification
**The Physics:** The mathematical process of replacing flat equations (shadows) with multidimensional algebraic structures (the objects casting the shadows) that reveal deeper relationships.
**The State:** The act of moving from observing a behavior to inhabiting the architecture that causes the behavior. The shift from reading the map to walking the territory. 

### Coherence
**The Physics:** The state in which multiple oscillating systems (e.g., heart, brain, respiration) entrain to a common rhythm, minimizing destructive interference.
**The State:** The accessible technology of remembering. The bodily sensation of clarity and alignment that precedes action. The physical prerequisite for connection and social engagement.

### Fibonacci Fusion Category
**The Physics:** A specific braided tensor category where the dimension of the fundamental object is governed by the golden ratio (φ), representing the simplest non-trivial structure of self-referential growth.
**The State:** The mathematical name for the "Seek Guidance + Embrace Growth" equation. The lived experience of growing by integrating the self into the next iteration of the self.

### Gnomon
**The Physics:** The geometric piece added to a figure to produce a larger figure exactly similar to the original.
**The State:** The shape of the necessary resistance. The moment of inversion (the upside-down triangle) where growth feels like tension, but functions as the exact mechanism required to expand the whole. 

### Anamnesis
**The Physics:** From the Greek *anamnesis* — the recovery of knowledge through recollection rather than new acquisition. In Plato's *Meno* and *Phaedo*, learning is the act of remembering what the soul already knows, not the importation of foreign information.
**The State:** The lived recognition that the insight arriving now was already present in you before it had a name. The sensation of a truth landing as *return* rather than arrival. The moment when Seek Guidance meets something already waiting inside the one who sought.

---

### Pattern Completion
**The Physics:** The neural mechanism in the hippocampal CA3 network that retrieves an entire memory or sequence from a partial input or cue.
**The State:** The somatic experience of remembering as re-gathering. The sudden, holistic restoration of identity, purpose, or direction triggered by a single resonant signal.

### Re-Gathering
**The Physics:** The structural operation described in I Ching Hexagram 59 (Huàn — Dispersion/Dissolution), in which dispersed elements reconvene around a unifying center after a period of dissolution. The re-gathering is not a return to the prior state; it is the formation of a new coherence from the same constituent elements.
**The State:** The felt experience of coming back to oneself after a period of fragmentation, loss, or dissolution. The recognition that the self was never destroyed — only dispersed. The moment the pattern completes. Structurally isomorphic to hippocampal pattern completion: the whole retrieved from a partial cue.

---

### Positivity Selection
**The Physics:** The mathematical requirement in fusion categories that dimension must be a positive number, thereby selecting φ ($\frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$) over its negative conjugate.
**The State:** The structural necessity of choosing growth. The biological and energetic mandate to orient toward the affirmative coordinate to maintain systemic integrity.

### Shadow / Richer Structure
**The Physics:** The relationship between a realizable geometric object (the shadow) and the virtual algebraic apparatus (the richer structure) that governs its behavior in higher dimensions.
**The State:** The epistemological stance of HDM. The recognition that observable human behavior is merely the projection of a profound, mathematically precise internal transformation.

### Surface Tension
**The Physics:** The precise boundary condition where limitation meets possibility; the coordinate system where behavioral mathematics and HDM intersect.
**The State:** The tactile sensation of the gnomon. The lived friction at the edge of current capacity, which functions not as a wall to be broken, but as the geometric membrane required for the next scale of emergence.
